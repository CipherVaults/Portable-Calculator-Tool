Controls are carried over from Date-Calculator-Tool v1.2.0 and Percentage-Calculator-Tool v2.3.0 and extended by the approved plan (B1–B3, B10, B14, B16). Each row moves from **planned** to **implemented** to **verified** with a Ledger evidence ID. **verified** means verified locally on the build machine (Chromium in headless Edge 157 and WebView2 only); Safari/iOS, Firefox and real phones are owner checks ([Acceptance Record](ACCEPTANCE_RECORD.md#-owner-checks-outstanding)). As of 2026-11-20, `dev` at `d7a6712`; CI run 44779154165 on it was green on all three jobs. The live CI and remote state are owned by [Current State](CURRENT_STATE.md#present-state-identity).
